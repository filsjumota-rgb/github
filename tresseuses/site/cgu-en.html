Terms of use

Last updated: 27 November 2026

1. The service

Tresseuses du Cameroun is an online directory. It lets braiders present their services (styles, indicative prices, photos, availability) and lets clients find them and contact them directly, by WhatsApp or by phone.

Using the directory is free. Optional paid services may be offered to braiders (for example better visibility, “Premium”). They are always announced clearly in advance and are never compulsory.

2. Our role

The site connects people. It does not do the hairstyles, does not set prices and does not receive payment for the services. The service, price, place, time and payment are agreed directly between the client and the braider.

We therefore cannot be held responsible for the quality of a service, a delay, a cancellation or a disagreement about the price. If there is a problem, please leave an honest review and, if necessary, report it to us.

3. Braiders' commitments

  • Give accurate information: name, city, neighbourhood, contact number and indicative prices.
  • Only post photos of their own work.
  • Have the consent of anyone recognisable in the photos, and never post a photo of a child without the parents' consent.
  • Not post shocking, misleading or off-topic content.
  • Keep their password secret. Each braider is responsible for what is posted from her account.

4. Client reviews

Reviews must describe a real experience, stay polite and contain no insults, phone numbers or personal information about another person. A braider cannot review her own profile.

We may remove a fake, abusive or off-topic review.

5. Premium subscription

Premium is paid by Mobile Money (MTN MoMo or Orange Money) for a fixed period shown before payment. It is not renewed automatically. It places the profile at the top of the results and adds a “★ Premium” badge; it does not guarantee a number of clients.

6. Moderation

We may edit or remove a profile, photo or review that does not respect these terms or Cameroonian law, and suspend an account in case of repeated abuse.

7. Deleting a profile

A braider can remove her profile at any time from the “I'm a braider” area, “Remove my profile” button. Her photos, reviews and statistics are then deleted.

8. Availability

We do our best to keep the site running at all times, but cannot guarantee it. It may be interrupted for maintenance or if one of our technical providers has an outage.

These terms may change. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page. By continuing to use the site after a change, you accept the new version.

10. Applicable law and contact

These terms are governed by Cameroonian law. The French version prevails in case of disagreement between the two versions.
