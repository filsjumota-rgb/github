Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This page explains what information we store, why, who can see it and how to delete it.

1. What we store

For braiders who create a profile:

  • the account email address and password (the password is encrypted and we cannot read it);
  • the profile information: display name, city, neighbourhood, WhatsApp number, years of experience, availability, description, styles and prices;
  • the photos of work added;
  • for Premium: the Mobile Money number used and the payment reference, amount and status.

For clients who leave a review: the first name given, the rating and the comment. No account is needed.

Statistics: when someone views a profile or taps “WhatsApp” or “Call”, we only record the type of action, the profile concerned and the date. We do not keep the name or number of the person viewing.

2. What is public

The purpose of the directory is to be seen. All visitors can therefore see: the profile information (including the WhatsApp number), the photos, and the reviews with their author's first name.

Not public: braiders' email addresses, statistics and payments. Each braider only sees her own statistics and payments.

3. Why

  • To show the directory and let clients contact braiders.
  • To let each braider edit her profile.
  • To show each braider how many people viewed her profile.
  • To process Premium payments.

We do not sell this information and do not use it for advertising.

4. Where the data is stored

Data is hosted by Supabase on servers in the European Union (Ireland). The website itself is hosted by GitHub Pages. Mobile Money payments are processed by Campay. These technical providers do not use your data for their own purposes.

On your phone, the site only keeps small practical details: your language choice, and whether you have already reviewed a braider or viewed a profile during your visit. There are no advertising cookies.

5. How long

Profile information is kept as long as the profile exists. When a braider removes her profile, her photos, the reviews she received and her statistics are deleted. Payment records may be kept for accounting purposes.

6. Your rights

You can view, correct or delete your information at any time:

  • Braiders: from the “I'm a braider” area (“Update my profile” or “Remove my profile”);
  • Clients: to have a review you left removed, contact us.
